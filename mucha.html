THE FIRST CHAPTER · 1890s

ART NOUVEAU

Alphonse Mucha

A world where nature becomes art, lines come alive, and beauty finds its rhythm.

Emerging in Europe at the turn of the 20th century, Art Nouveau transformed everyday objects, architecture and illustration through flowing organic lines, floral motifs and decorative elegance. Alphonse Mucha became one of its most celebrated artists, known for graceful female figures, intricate ornaments and harmonious compositions.
